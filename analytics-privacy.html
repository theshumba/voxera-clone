Site analytics

We use PostHog to count page visits and clicks, and to see broad information such as country and which pages people use. We use these totals to improve this site. We do not record form entries or replay your visit.

This site uses a shared PostHog project hosted in the EU. A temporary ID stays in your browser tab while it is open. We do not use analytics cookies. PostHog's free plan retains events for one year. Your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected.

You can turn this site's analytics off here. Your choice is saved in this browser for this site. It does not change analytics on other websites.
